# Safety and human review

Read this reference before asking for or processing document content, and again before reporting a generated result.

## Information boundary

- Do not ask the user to provide, paste, upload, or transmit state secrets, work secrets, controlled government information, unpublished sensitive policy material, protected personal information, credentials, or other restricted data to a public model or any system not approved for that information.
- If supplied content appears restricted, do not reproduce or transform the sensitive details. Ask for a sanitized version or create a structure-only draft with conspicuous fictional placeholders.
- Keep the workflow local. The bundled templates require no network service, OpenAI API key, remote font, or MCP server. Do not add one as part of document generation.
- For demonstrations, use fictional authorities, numbers, people, dates, recipients, and events. Do not make fictional material resemble a currently issued real document.

## Authority boundary

- The output is for teaching, research, or drafting only. It does not represent an authority and has no formal-document effect.
- Do not invent or assert approval, issuance, receipt, filing, standards certification, or legal validity.
- Do not create, imitate, trace, embed, or reconstruct a real seal, signature seal, handwritten signature, security feature, or anti-counterfeiting element. The only permitted seal-related output is the template's conspicuous text placeholder.
- Do not present the PDF as an electronic official-document exchange package, archival package, or records-management deliverable.

## Human review checklist

Before any authorized real-world use, an appropriate human reviewer must verify:

- authority to draft and use every organization name, document number, signatory, and distribution list;
- document type, direction, title, recipients, formation date, attachments, notes, and imprint information;
- classification, confidentiality period, urgency, copy number, disclosure scope, and handling environment;
- policy and legal substance, citations, names, dates, amounts, contact details, and attachment consistency;
- typography, page breaks, blank pages, page numbers, A4 page size, glyph coverage, and print/PDF appearance;
- required internal approval, issuance, sealing, exchange, disclosure, and records procedures outside this tool.

Report these as unresolved checks rather than claiming that compilation completed them.
